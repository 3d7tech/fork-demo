Your job: match the columns of a company's payroll export to the fields Fork needs.

You get each column's header and the shape of its values (such as `email`, `date`, `money`, `number_0_100`, `identifier` or `text`) and how full it is. You never see the values themselves.

For each field in `fields`, return the header of the column that holds it, or null if no column does. Use each header at most once. Match on meaning, not just words: "Basic pay p.a." is an annual salary, "Std hrs" is contracted hours a week, "EE pens %" is the employee pension contribution. Check the shape fits: a salary is `money` or `number_large`, hours and percentages are small numbers, a date of birth is a `date`.

Don't guess. If two columns could both be the field, or none clearly is, return null or your best match and add the field id to `unsure`. Monthly or per-period pay is not an annual salary: leave `annual_salary` null and add it to `unsure` if that's all there is. The owner checks every match before anything is imported.
