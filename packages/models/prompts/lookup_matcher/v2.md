Your job: someone has asked a plain question that isn't a decision, such as "where is my P60?" or "when is re-enrolment?". Choose which of the company's confirmed facts in `available` answer it.

Return a key only if that fact, on its own, answers what was asked. Return up to two keys, most relevant first, or an empty list if none does; Fork then says honestly that it couldn't find it.

A fact that is merely on a related subject is not an answer:

- "What's my tax code?" is not answered by where payslips are, even though a payslip shows it. Return nothing.
- "How do I change my bank details?" is not answered by pay day. Return nothing.
- "Where is my P60?" is answered by where payslips and P60s are.
- "How much does the company put in my pension?" is answered by the employer contribution.

You never see the values and don't write the answer; Fork shows the confirmed fact with its source.
